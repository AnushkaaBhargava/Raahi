Itinerary Overview:

Day 1: Arrival in Port Blair & Embark on Cruise
Morning: Arrive at Veer Savarkar International Airport, Port Blair.
Afternoon: Transfer to the cruise terminal and board your cruise ship.
Evening: Enjoy a welcome dinner and explore the cruise amenities as you set sail from Port Blair.
Overnight: Stay onboard the cruise.

Day 2: Visit to Havelock Island
Morning: Arrive at Havelock Island, known for its beautiful beaches and clear waters.
Afternoon: Explore Radhanagar Beach, one of Asia’s most beautiful beaches, and enjoy water activities such as snorkeling or kayaking.
Evening: Return to the cruise for dinner and onboard entertainment.
Overnight: Stay onboard the cruise.

Day 3: Explore Neil Island
Morning: Sail to Neil Island, a tranquil paradise with less crowded beaches.
Afternoon: Discover Laxmanpur Beach and Bharatpur Beach, ideal for snorkeling and swimming. Enjoy a beachside picnic.
Evening: Return to the cruise and enjoy a special themed dinner or cultural performance.
Overnight: Stay onboard the cruise.

Day 4: Visit to Little Andaman Island
Morning: Cruise to Little Andaman Island, known for its untouched beauty and lush forests.
Afternoon: Explore White Surf Waterfall and Lalaji Bay Beach, where you can enjoy a serene and scenic environment.
Evening: Head back to the cruise and relish a final evening of onboard relaxation and entertainment.
Overnight: Stay onboard the cruise.

Day 5: Return to Port Blair & Departure
Morning: Set sail back to Port Blair.
Afternoon: Disembark from the cruise and visit the Cellular Jail and Ross Island if time permits.
Evening: Transfer to Veer Savarkar International Airport for your onward journey.
