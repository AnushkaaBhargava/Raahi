112 App Information

About the 112 App

The 112 app is a critical safety tool designed to assist you in emergencies. By using this app, you can quickly contact emergency services such as police, medical, and fire services with just a few taps. The app provides real-time location tracking, ensuring that help can reach you quickly.

Features of the 112 App

  • Emergency Calls: Contact emergency services instantly with the press of a button.
  • Real-time Location Sharing: Share your location with emergency responders for faster assistance.
  • Emergency Alerts: Send alerts to family and friends with your current location and situation.
  • Useful Information: Access emergency contacts, safety tips, and more within the app.

Safety Tips

Research your destination's local laws and customs.

Keep copies of important documents, such as your passport, in a safe place.

Avoid displaying expensive items in public.

Stay aware of your surroundings, especially in crowded areas.

Use reputable transportation services.

Purchase travel insurance before your trip.

Follow health guidelines and stay updated on travel advisories.
