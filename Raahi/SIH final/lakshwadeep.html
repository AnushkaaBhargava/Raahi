Lakshadweep Escape: 5-Day Tropical Cruise Adventure

Starting From: Rs. 37769/- per person

Discover the pristine beauty and hidden gems of Lakshadweep with our “Lakshadweep Escape” package. This 5-day adventure combines a luxurious cruise experience with visits to some of the archipelago's less-explored islands. Enjoy a budget-friendly yet unforgettable tropical getaway that offers a unique alternative to the Maldives.

Itinerary Overview:

Day 1: Arrival in Kochi & Embark on the Cruise
Morning: Arrive at Cochin International Airport, Kochi.
Afternoon: Transfer to the cruise terminal and board the cruise ship.
Evening: Set sail from Kochi to Lakshadweep. Enjoy onboard activities and entertainment.
Overnight: Stay onboard the cruise.

Day 2: Arrival at Kavaratti Island
Morning: Arrive at Kavaratti Island, the administrative capital of Lakshadweep.
Afternoon: Explore Kavaratti’s lesser-known Coral Garden and enjoy a snorkeling session to discover vibrant marine life.
Evening: Relax on the serene beaches or take part in a cultural performance onboard.
Overnight: Stay onboard the cruise.

Day 3: Visit to Kalapeni Island
Morning: Sail to Kalapeni Island, known for its stunning coral reefs and secluded beaches.
Afternoon: Enjoy activities such as kayaking, stand-up paddleboarding, and snorkeling in the pristine waters of Kalapeni.
Evening: Return to the cruise for dinner and evening entertainment.
Overnight: Stay onboard the cruise.

Day 4: Explore Suheli Atoll
Morning: Cruise to Suheli Atoll, a less-explored gem known for its untouched beauty and rich marine ecosystem.
Afternoon: Engage in snorkeling, scuba diving, or simply relax on the island’s quiet beaches.
Evening: Enjoy a beachside BBQ dinner or a special themed evening on the cruise.
Overnight: Stay onboard the cruise.

Day 5: Return to Kochi & Departure
Morning: Set sail back to Kochi.
Afternoon: Disembark from the cruise and transfer to Cochin International Airport.
Evening: Depending on your flight schedule, enjoy some leisure time in Kochi or head directly to the airport for your onward journey.
