Daman & Diu Delight: 3-Day Coastal Escape

Discover the charm and beauty of the coastal gems of Daman and Diu with our “Daman & Diu Delight” package. This 3-day getaway is perfect for those looking to unwind by the beach, explore historic sites, and indulge in some leisurely activities. Enjoy a relaxed yet enriching experience in these picturesque Union Territories.

Itinerary Overview:

Day 1: Arrival in Daman
Morning: Arrive at Daman's Vapi Railway Station or Daman Airport and transfer to your hotel.
Afternoon: Check-in at your hotel and freshen up.
Evening: Explore Jampore Beach or Devka Beach, known for their tranquil waters and beautiful sunsets.
Overnight: Stay at The Deltin or Hotel Sangam.

Day 2: Daman to Diu (90 km, 2-3 hours)
Morning: After breakfast, drive to Diu, a charming island with a rich colonial heritage.
Afternoon: Check-in at your hotel in Diu. Start your exploration with a visit to the Diu Fort, offering panoramic views of the Arabian Sea.
Evening: Visit Nagoa Beach for a relaxing evening by the sea and enjoy a beachside dinner.
Overnight: Stay at Hotel Apana or The Hoka Hotel.

Day 3: Explore Diu & Departure
Morning: After breakfast, explore more of Diu. Visit the St. Paul's Church, known for its exquisite Portuguese architecture, and the Diu Museum.
Afternoon: Spend some time at Goghla Beach or explore the Gangeshwar Temple, known for its rock-cut Shiva Lingas.
Evening: Return to Daman or proceed to your next destination.
