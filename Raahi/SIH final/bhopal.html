Indore, Ujjain & Bhopal: The Central India Heritage Tour

Starting From: Rs. 10450/- per person

Discover the cultural richness and historical treasures of Central India with our “Indore, Ujjain & Bhopal: The Central India Heritage Tour.” This 4-day journey will take you through the vibrant cityscapes and sacred sites of Indore, Ujjain, and Bhopal, offering a blend of spiritual exploration, historical landmarks, and local flavors.

Itinerary Overview:

Day 1: Arrival in Indore
Morning: Arrive in Indore and check into your hotel.
Afternoon: Visit the Rajwada Palace and Lal Baag Palace, exploring Indore's royal heritage.
Evening: Explore the bustling Sarafa Bazar for local street food and shopping.
Overnight: Stay at Radisson Blu Indore or Hotel Sayaji.

Day 2: Indore to Ujjain (55 km, 1-2 hours)
Morning: Drive to Ujjain, a significant pilgrimage city.
Late Morning: Visit the Mahakaleshwar Temple, one of the twelve Jyotirlingas.
Afternoon: Explore other notable temples including Harsiddhi Temple and Kal Bhairav Temple.
Evening: Experience the Kshipra River Ganga Aarti and enjoy the serene atmosphere.
Overnight: Stay at Hotel Anjushree or Ujjain Ashray Hotel.

Day 3: Ujjain to Bhopal (190 km, 4-5 hours)
Morning: Depart for Bhopal, the capital city of Madhya Pradesh.
Afternoon: Visit the Bhojpur Temple, renowned for its massive Shivling and historical significance.
Late Afternoon: Explore the Upper Lake and Lower Lake, enjoying the scenic views.
Evening: Discover Raja Bhoj’s Palace and relax in the evening.
Overnight: Stay at Jehan Numa Palace Hotel or Hotel The Residency.

Day 4: Explore Bhopal & Departure
Morning: Visit the Sanchi Stupa, a UNESCO World Heritage site, known for its ancient Buddhist architecture.
Afternoon: Explore the Bhopal National Museum and Gohar Mahal.
Late Afternoon: Depending on your departure time, explore local markets or relax.
Evening: Check out from the hotel and proceed to the airport or train station for your onward journey.
