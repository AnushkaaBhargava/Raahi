Jodhpur: The Blue City Heritage Tour

Starting From: Rs. 11500/- per person

Experience the vibrant charm and rich history of Jodhpur, the Blue City of Rajasthan. Our “Jodhpur: The Blue City Heritage Tour” package offers a blend of cultural exploration, historical grandeur, and traditional Rajasthani hospitality over 4 days and 3 nights.

Itinerary Overview:

Day 1: Arrival in Jodhpur
Morning: Arrive in Jodhpur and check into your hotel.
Afternoon: Explore the bustling Clock Tower Market, where you can shop for local handicrafts and spices.
Evening: Enjoy a traditional Rajasthani dinner at a local restaurant.
Overnight: Stay at Umaid Bhawan Palace or Ratan Vilas.

Day 2: Explore Jodhpur’s Forts and Palaces
Morning: Visit the majestic Mehrangarh Fort, one of the largest forts in India, and explore its museum and courtyards.
Afternoon: Discover Jaswant Thada, the beautiful white marble cenotaph built in honor of Maharaja Jaswant Singh II.
Evening: Stroll through the vibrant Sardar Market and experience local life.
Overnight: Return to your hotel for an overnight stay.

Day 3: Excursion to Osian
Morning: Take a day trip to Osian, an ancient town known for its stunning temples and desert landscapes.
Afternoon: Visit the Sachiya Mata Temple and Mahavira Jain Temple.
Evening: Return to Jodhpur and enjoy a relaxing evening. Optionally, experience a cultural evening with traditional music and dance.
Overnight: Stay at your hotel in Jodhpur.

Day 4: Departure
Morning: Enjoy a leisurely breakfast at the hotel.
Late Morning: Depending on your departure time, explore any missed attractions or relax before your journey.
Afternoon: Check out and proceed to the airport or train station for your onward journey.
