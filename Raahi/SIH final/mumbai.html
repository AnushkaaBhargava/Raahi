Mumbai To Pune: The Ultimate Road Odyssey

Embark on an exciting road trip from Mumbai to Pune with our “Mumbai To Pune: The Ultimate Road Odyssey” package. Over 4 days, experience scenic drives, cultural explorations, and vibrant city life as you journey through some of Western India's most beautiful destinations.

Itinerary Overview:

Day 1: Mumbai to Lonavala (75 km, 2 hours)
Morning: Begin your adventure with an early departure from Mumbai.
Late Morning: Drive through the picturesque Ghats to Lonavala, a charming hill station renowned for its natural beauty.
Afternoon: Explore local attractions such as the scenic viewpoints, ancient forts, and enchanting caves.
Evening: Check into Della Resorts for a luxurious overnight stay amidst lush surroundings.

Day 2: Lonavala to Pune (60 km, 1 hour 30 minutes)
Morning: Continue your journey from Lonavala to Pune.
Late Morning: Arrive in Pune and delve into its vibrant culture and history.
Afternoon: Visit the iconic Shaniwar Wada and other local landmarks.
Evening: Relax at The O Hotel in Pune, enjoying modern amenities and comfort.

Day 3: Pune Exploration
Morning: Discover Pune’s rich heritage with visits to the Aga Khan Palace, National Film Archives of India, and Raja Dinkar Kelkar Museum.
Afternoon: Enjoy a leisurely walk through Pune's bustling streets and lively markets.
Evening: Participate in a campfire meal and soak in the ambiance of Pune.

Day 4: Pune to Mumbai (175 km, 4 hours)
Morning: Start your drive back to Mumbai, taking in the scenic beauty of the Ghats once more.
Afternoon: Arrive in Mumbai by the end of the day, concluding your memorable road odyssey.
