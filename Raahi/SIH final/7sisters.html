Seven Sisters Adventure: 7-Day Northeast India Exploration

Starting From: Rs. 33333/- per person

Embark on an unforgettable journey through the mesmerizing "Seven Sisters" of Northeast India with our “Seven Sisters Adventure” package. Over 7 days, explore the stunning landscapes, rich cultures, and vibrant traditions of Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Nagaland, Manipur, Mizoram, Tripura, and Meghalaya.

Itinerary Overview:

Day 1: Arrival in Guwahati, Assam
*Morning:* Arrive at Lokpriya Gopinath Bordoloi International Airport, Guwahati.
*Afternoon:* Transfer to your hotel and check-in.
*Evening:* Explore the local markets and visit the *Kamakhya Temple*.
*Overnight:* Stay at *Radisson Blu Guwahati* or *Hotel Nandan*.

Day 2: Guwahati to Shillong, Meghalaya (100 km, 3 hours)
*Morning:* Drive to Shillong, the "Scotland of the East".
*Afternoon:* Visit the *Elephant Falls* and *Shillong Peak* for panoramic views.
*Evening:* Stroll around *Police Bazaar* for local shopping and dining.
*Overnight:* Stay at *Hotel Polo Towers* or *Shillong Guest House*.

Day 3: Shillong to Cherrapunjee (60 km, 2 hours)
*Morning:* Depart for Cherrapunjee, famous for its living root bridges and waterfalls.
*Afternoon:* Explore the *Nohkalikai Falls* and *Mawsmai Caves*.
*Evening:* Return to Shillong for the night.
*Overnight:* Stay at *Hotel Polo Towers* or similar.

Day 4: Shillong to Kaziranga National Park, Assam (180 km, 4-5 hours)
*Morning:* Travel to Kaziranga National Park, known for its one-horned rhinoceroses.
*Afternoon:* Enjoy a jeep safari in the park to spot wildlife.
*Evening:* Relax and enjoy a traditional Assamese dinner.
*Overnight:* Stay at *Diphlu River Lodge* or *Kaziranga Resort*.

Day 5: Kaziranga to Kohima, Nagaland (200 km, 6-7 hours)
*Morning:* Depart for Kohima, the capital of Nagaland.
*Afternoon:* Arrive in Kohima and check into your hotel.
*Evening:* Visit the *Kohima War Cemetery* and explore local markets.
*Overnight:* Stay at *Hotel Japfu* or *The Heritage*.

Day 6: Kohima to Imphal, Manipur (100 km, 3-4 hours)
*Morning:* Drive to Imphal, the capital of Manipur.
*Afternoon:* Visit the *Imphal War Cemetery*, *Kangla Fort*, and the *Loktak Lake*.
*Evening:* Enjoy local Manipuri cuisine and culture.
*Overnight:* Stay at *Classic Hotel* or *Hotel Imphal*.

Day 7: Imphal to Guwahati (550 km, 8-9 hours)
*Morning:* Early morning drive or flight back to Guwahati.
*Afternoon:* Arrive in Guwahati and enjoy some free time for last-minute shopping or sightseeing.
*Evening:* Check out from the hotel and transfer to the airport for your onward journey.
