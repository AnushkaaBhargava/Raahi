Kerala Bliss: 5-Day Enchantment in God's Own Country

Starting From: Rs. 19359/- per person

Immerse yourself in the serene beauty and rich culture of Kerala with our “Kerala Bliss” package. This 5-day tour covers the highlights of this tropical paradise, offering a mix of lush landscapes, tranquil backwaters, and vibrant local culture.

Itinerary Overview:

Day 1: Arrival in Kochi
Morning: Arrive at Cochin International Airport and transfer to your hotel in Kochi.
Afternoon: Explore the historic Fort Kochi area, visiting attractions such as the Chinese Fishing Nets, St. Francis Church, and the Dutch Palace.
Evening: Enjoy a traditional Kathakali dance performance at a local cultural center.
Overnight: Stay at Brunton Boatyard or The Malabar House.

Day 2: Kochi to Munnar (130 km, 4-5 hours)
Morning: Depart for Munnar, the picturesque hill station renowned for its tea plantations.
Afternoon: Visit the Tea Museum and explore the lush tea gardens.
Evening: Enjoy the scenic beauty of Mattupetty Dam and Echo Point.
Overnight: Stay at Tea County or Grand Plaza.

Day 3: Munnar to Thekkady (90 km, 3-4 hours)
Morning: Drive to Thekkady, famous for its wildlife sanctuary and spice plantations.
Afternoon: Take a boat ride on Periyar Lake within the Periyar Wildlife Sanctuary to spot wildlife such as elephants and deer.
Evening: Explore local spice markets and enjoy a traditional Kerala meal.
Overnight: Stay at Cardamom County or Spice Village.

Day 4: Thekkady to Alleppey (130 km, 4-5 hours)
Morning: Transfer to Alleppey, the heart of Kerala's backwater region.
Afternoon: Board a traditional houseboat for a relaxing cruise through the backwaters of Kerala.
Evening: Enjoy a freshly prepared Kerala meal on board and take in the serene views of the waterways and lush landscapes.
Overnight: Stay overnight on the houseboat.

Day 5: Alleppey to Kochi & Departure
Morning: Disembark from the houseboat and drive back to Kochi.
Afternoon: Enjoy some leisure time for last-minute shopping or sightseeing in Kochi.
Evening: Check out from the hotel and transfer to Cochin International Airport for your onward journey.
