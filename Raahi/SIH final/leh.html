Leh Dreams: Manali to Leh Scenic Trek

Starting From: Rs. 14650/- per person

Embark on an unforgettable journey through the breathtaking landscapes of the Indian Himalayas with our “Leh Dreams: Manali to Leh Scenic Trek” package. This 9-day adventure takes you from the charming town of Manali to the mystical landscapes of Leh and Nubra Valley, featuring high-altitude passes, stunning lakes, and ancient monasteries.

Itinerary Overview:

Day 1: Manali to Rohtang Pass (51 km, 1-2 hours)
Morning: Depart from Manali and begin your journey through the scenic Rohtang Pass.
Afternoon: Cross the Rohtang Pass at 13,050 ft and arrive in the quaint village of Kokang.
Evening: Overnight stay in Kokang.

Day 2: Kokang to Keylong (100 km, 4-5 hours)
Morning: Continue your journey towards Keylong.
Afternoon: Explore the Bara Lacha Pass at 16,390 ft and the picturesque Lahaul Valley.
Evening: Arrival in Keylong and overnight stay.

Day 3: Keylong to Sarchu (70 km, 5-6 hours)
Morning: Head towards Sarchu, crossing the Tanglang La Pass (17,380 ft) and the Nakee La Pass (15,480 ft).
Afternoon: Enjoy the stunning high-altitude desert of Sarchu.
Evening: Overnight stay in Sarchu.

Day 4: Sarchu to Pangong Lake (140 km, 6-7 hours)
Morning: Start your journey to the stunning Pangong Lake.
Afternoon: Cross the Chang La Pass (17,370 ft) and reach Pangong Lake.
Evening: Relax by the lake, enjoy a campfire, and overnight stay.

Day 5: Pangong Lake to Leh (140 km, 5-6 hours)
Morning: Explore the beautiful Pangong Lake with options for boating or trekking.
Afternoon: Begin your drive towards Leh.
Evening: Arrive in Leh and check into your hotel.

Day 6: Explore Leh
Morning: Discover Leh’s historical sites and ancient monasteries.
Afternoon: Visit the Shanti Stupa for panoramic views of Leh.
Evening: Leisure time to explore local markets and enjoy a meal.

Day 7: Leh to Nubra Valley (125 km, 4-5 hours)
Morning: Depart for Nubra Valley, crossing the Khardung La Pass.
Afternoon: Arrive in Diskit, the main town of Nubra Valley.
Evening: Overnight stay in Nubra Valley.

Day 8: Explore Nubra Valley
Morning: Explore the stunning Nubra Valley landscapes.
Afternoon: Visit Diskit Monastery and the Hunder Sand Dunes.
Evening: Enjoy a relaxing evening in Nubra Valley.

Day 9: Nubra Valley to Manali (125 km, 4-5 hours)
Morning: Start your journey back to Manali.
Afternoon: Cross the Khardung La Pass, Chang La Pass, Tanglang La Pass, and Rohtang Pass.
Evening: Arrive back in Manali and conclude your trek.
